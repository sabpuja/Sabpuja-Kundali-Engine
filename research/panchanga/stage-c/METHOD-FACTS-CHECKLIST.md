# Stage C method facts checklist, Part A (blind)

**BLANK TEMPLATE. To be completed by the appointed verifier from the official IMD pages only.**

These checks record what IMD says about its own methods and names. They are **not** members of the 46-record dataset and do not change its count. Part B, [METHOD-FACTS-RECONCILIATION.md](METHOD-FACTS-RECONCILIATION.md), holds the facts already reported by the Stage A/B reviewer and the conventions under consideration. Do not open Part B until this part is frozen.

For each item, write one of:

- **STATED**: quote the shortest exact phrase that answers it, with the page URL and the heading or paragraph where it appears;
- **NOT STATED**: the publication does not answer it;
- **UNCLEAR**: the publication addresses it but the meaning is ambiguous (explain).

Never fill an item from general knowledge, another publication or a calculation.
