# IMD source rights checklist

**Facts to record and questions to answer. No legal conclusion is made here, and none should be drawn from this file.**

Public access to a government website does not by itself grant unrestricted republication rights. Until this checklist is reviewed by the owner and, where needed, a legal specialist, the project records only minimal individual factual fields from IMD and commits no page text, screenshots, PDFs or tables.

## C. Questions for specialist legal review

1. Does the rights notice cover individual factual values (an ending time, a name) as well as the publication's text and layout?
2. Is limited extraction of numerical facts distinguishable, under applicable Indian and other relevant law, from reproducing protected publication content?
3. Does any government open-data policy apply to the Rashtriya Panchang web edition?
4. May the project store the 46 values, with attribution, in a public or private repository? Under what attribution wording?
5. May verified values be used as engineering test references in software distributed under AGPL-3.0, and may they appear in customer-facing reports?
6. Is written permission from the publisher advisable before Stage D, and who should request it?
