Why an exact time is needed

The Lagna (ascendant) moves through a whole sign in about two hours, and the twelve houses and the D9 Lagna all follow from it. A guessed time could give a different Lagna, so this prototype never assumes midnight, noon or any other time.

Please check a birth certificate, hospital record or family record, then enter the time here. A chart without a birth time is not part of this prototype.

Method and transparency

What this prototype does not calculate

  • Births before 1970-01-01: historical clock records are not yet verified, so these are refused.
  • Local dates after 2100-12-31.
  • Latitudes beyond 66° north or south.
  • Unknown birth times: no time is ever assumed.
  • Local times that never happened (clocks moved forward); repeated times need your choice.
  • Places outside the curated list.
