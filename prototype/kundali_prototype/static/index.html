Why an exact time is needed

The Lagna (ascendant) moves through a whole sign in about two hours, and the twelve houses and the D9 Lagna all follow from it. A guessed time could give a different Lagna, so this preview never assumes midnight, noon or any other time.

Please check a birth certificate, hospital record or family record, then enter the time here. A chart without a birth time is not available in this preview.

Key placements

D1 Rashi chart North Indian style

Houses are fixed with the Lagna (Asc) at the top; numbers are signs (1 Mesha … 12 Meena). Su Sun, Mo Moon, Ma Mars, Me Mercury, Ju Jupiter, Ve Venus, Sa Saturn, Ra Rahu, Ke Ketu.

D1 placements

D1 placements: sidereal longitude, rashi and whole-sign house of the Lagna and each graha
GrahaLongitudeRashiHouse

Sidereal longitudes (Lahiri), Rahu and Ketu by the mean node. Longitudes are the engine's 8-decimal display values. Rashi and house come from the unrounded values, so a value shown as exactly 30° can still belong to the earlier sign.

D9 Navamsa chart North Indian style

D9 houses are whole-sign houses counted from the D9 Lagna (Asc, House 1, at the top); numbers are D9 signs (1 Mesha … 12 Meena). They are not the D1 houses. Su Sun, Mo Moon, Ma Mars, Me Mercury, Ju Jupiter, Ve Venus, Sa Saturn, Ra Rahu, Ke Ketu.

D9 Navamsa placements

D9 Navamsa sign and D9 whole-sign house of the D9 Lagna and each graha

D9 signs come from the unrounded sidereal longitudes in 3°20′ divisions; D9 houses are counted from the D9 Lagna sign, so a D9 house number is not a D1 house number. The D9 house fields use the owner-approved chart schema 0.3.0.

Method and transparency

What this private preview does not calculate

  • Births before 1970-01-01: historical clock records are not yet verified, so these are refused.
  • Local dates after 2100-12-31.
  • Latitudes beyond 66° north or south.
  • Unknown birth times: no time is ever assumed.
  • Local times that never happened (clocks moved forward); repeated times need your choice.
  • Places outside the curated list.
